About Selkeramik

Selkeramik is a miniature ceramic brand focused on handcrafted forms, earthy textures, and timeless design. Each piece is made slowly—small but meaningful—embracing imperfection through delicate forms and material honesty.

Process

Every piece is wheel-thrown, trimmed, fired, and glazed by hand. The process values patience, repetition, and material exploration.
